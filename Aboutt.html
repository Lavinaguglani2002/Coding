About Our Project

Smart Attendance Tracker System is a simple web-based project designed to make student attendance management easier and more organized.

It allows users to add student details, mark attendance, edit or delete student records, search for students and view attendance summaries.

Our Main Features

Student Management

Add, edit, delete and search student records.

Attendance Management

Mark students as Present or Absent.

Attendance Summary

View the total number of students, present students and absent students.

Local Storage

Save student records in the browser using JavaScript LocalStorage.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple and user-friendly way to maintain student attendance records digitally.
